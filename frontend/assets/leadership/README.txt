Leadership photos
==================

Drop official, properly-licensed photos here with these exact filenames,
and the homepage's Leadership section will use them automatically — no
code changes needed. Anyone missing just falls back to a plain initials
badge, so it's safe to add these one at a time.

  president.jpg   — President of the Republic
  vp.jpg          — Vice President
  minister.jpg    — Minister of Education

Recommended: square or portrait crop, at least 400x400px, under ~500KB
each (JPEG). These are NOT included in this build — they need to come
from an authorized source (e.g. the Information Services Department or
the Ministry's own press office) with the rights to use them here, since
official photos of sitting government officials are copyrighted assets.
